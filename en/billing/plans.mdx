You start for free with **Free** and can move up to a larger plan right from your
browser whenever you need to. Payments are handled by our payment provider
**Polar**.

## The plans

| Plan | Price / month | Agents | Conversation hours / month |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | €0 | 2 | 0.5 |
| **Starter** | €10 | 10 | 1 |
| **Team** | €40 | 50 | 5 |
| **Business** | €200 | 100 | 35 |
| **Scale** | €600 | 500 | 125 |
| **Enterprise** | custom | unlimited | custom |

Prices are final prices including VAT. With an **annual subscription** you pay 10
monthly prices for 12 months. The conversation hours apply to conversations with
an avatar; generated videos use the same allowance.

The [pricing page](https://avaluma.ai/pricing) is authoritative. It also lists all
features of each plan, such as concurrent sessions and prices for extra minutes.

## How many agents can I create?

Every plan allows a fixed number of agents to exist at the same time (see the
table above). Once that allowance is used up, the **New agent** button in the
Studio is disabled and shows how many of your agents you're using.

The limit applies to **every** way a new agent can come into existence:

- **New agent** via the button or the `/studio/agents/new` page
- **Duplicating** an existing agent
- **Transferring** an agent to you — if your allowance is full, the transfer
  fails and the sender sees a note

<Note>
  **Existing agents are never affected:** they always stay, even if you own more
  agents than your current plan allows (for example after a downgrade). Nothing
  is deleted and nothing is switched off; only creating **additional** agents is
  blocked until you're back below the limit or move to a larger plan.
</Note>

To create more agents, switch to a larger plan under
[Studio → Plan](https://avaluma.ai/studio/plan) — or delete an agent you no
longer need.

## Keeping an eye on usage

In the [Studio](https://avaluma.ai/studio/agents), the **Agents** page always
shows your current usage:

- One bar shows the **conversation minutes used in the current billing period**
  relative to your plan's limit (e.g. `16 / 30 min` on the Free plan).
- A second bar shows the **number of your agents** relative to your plan's agent
  allowance (e.g. `1 / 2 agents`).
- Each agent card additionally shows how many minutes **that agent** has been used
  in the current period.

<Note>
  The minutes display is for guidance only right now — conversations continue
  even once the limit is reached, and the counter resets automatically at the
  start of the new billing period. The **agent limit**, in contrast, is enforced.
</Note>

## Subscribing to a paid plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Open [Studio → Plan](https://avaluma.ai/studio/plan) or the
    [pricing page](https://avaluma.ai/pricing) and click the button of the plan
    you want, e.g. "Choose Team".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in or sign up">
    A paid plan is always tied to your Avaluma account. If you're not signed in
    yet, you'll be taken to sign in first and then returned to checkout
    automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay with Polar">
    You'll land on Polar's secure checkout page. Enter your payment details there
    and complete the purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Back in the Studio">
    After a successful payment you're returned to the **Studio** (agents page) and see a
    confirmation. Your plan is activated automatically once the payment is
    settled with Polar — this usually takes only a few seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Billing, your invoices, and managing or canceling your subscription are handled
  by **Polar**. After your purchase you'll receive a confirmation email from Polar
  with a link to your customer portal. You can also get there at any time with the
  **Payment method & invoices** button under
  [Studio → Plan](https://avaluma.ai/studio/plan).
</Note>

## Changing your plan

You change your plan under [Studio → Plan](https://avaluma.ai/studio/plan):

- **Larger plan (upgrade):** takes effect immediately. You only pay the difference
  for the rest of the current period.
- **Smaller plan (downgrade):** takes effect at the end of the current period.
  Until then you keep your current plan.
- **Month → year** counts as an upgrade and applies immediately, **year → month** as
  a downgrade at the end of the period.
