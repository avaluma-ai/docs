Avaluma offers three plans. You start for free with **Free** and can subscribe to
**Starter** or **Pro** right from your browser whenever you need to. Payments are
handled by our payment provider **Polar**.

## The plans

| Plan | Price | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | €0 – forever | Trying out the Agent Builder. No server, no credit card required. |
| **Starter** | €49 / month | Growing teams — more agents, conversation minutes and custom branding. |
| **Pro** | €199 / month | Businesses — unlimited agents, team access and priority support. |

You'll find the exact features of each plan in the pricing section on
[avaluma.ai](https://avaluma.ai/#pricing).

## Subscribing to a paid plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    In the pricing section on [avaluma.ai](https://avaluma.ai/#pricing), click the
    subscribe button on **Starter** or **Pro**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in or sign up">
    A paid plan is always tied to your Avaluma account. If you're not signed in
    yet, you'll be taken to sign in first and then returned to checkout
    automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay with Polar">
    You'll land on Polar's secure checkout page. Enter your payment details there
    and complete the purchase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Back in the dashboard">
    After a successful payment you're returned to the **dashboard** and see a
    confirmation. Your plan is activated automatically once the payment is
    settled with Polar — this usually takes only a few seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Billing, your invoices, and managing or canceling your subscription are handled
  by **Polar**. After your purchase you'll receive a confirmation email from Polar
  with a link to your customer portal, where you can view and manage your
  subscription at any time.
</Note>
