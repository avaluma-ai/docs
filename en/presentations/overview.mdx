With **Presentations** (beta) an Avaluma avatar presents your slides. You upload a presentation in the dashboard and send a link. Viewers see the slides and the avatar, which presents on its own, shows the right slide and answers questions at any time – by voice, in German or English.

## Create a presentation

<Steps>
  <Step title="New presentation">
    Open **Presentations** in the [dashboard](https://avaluma.ai/studio/slides) and click **New presentation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the file">
    Drag a file into the upload area or click it. The slides are then prepared – depending on the size this takes one to a few minutes. You can leave the page in the meantime; as soon as the status is **Ready**, the link works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settings">
    Set the **title**, **start language**, **avatar**, **voice** and how questions are answered (see [Answering questions](#answering-questions)), then click **Save**. Without a title, the title from the file is used. Voices are grouped into female and male and can be previewed with ▶ – pick one that matches the avatar. Every voice speaks German and English.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link">
    Under **Sharing**, copy the link (`https://avaluma.ai/slides/…`) and send it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supported formats

| Format | What is included |
|---|---|
| PPTX | Slides, text, **speaker notes** and embedded **videos** (hidden slides are skipped) |
| PDF | Slides and text |
| PPT, ODP | Slides and text, no notes |
| Keynote | Export as PowerPoint in Keynote (*File → Export To → PowerPoint*) and upload the PPTX |

Up to 200 MB per file. Animations and transitions are not included – each slide is shown as a still image.

<Tip>
  The avatar uses the slide text, speaker notes and an automatic image description of each slide. Good **speaker notes** are the most effective way to get a good talk.
</Tip>

To replace the presentation, upload a new file under **File**. The link stays the same.

## Review and edit slide content

Once the presentation is **Ready**, the **Slides** section shows each slide in a large view. Browse with the arrows, the arrow keys or the thumbnails below. Underneath the slide you see what the avatar knows about it – you can correct or extend all of it:

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Title | Slide title |
| Text on the slide | Slide text taken from the file |
| Visual content | Automatically generated description of images, charts and graphics |
| Speaker notes | Guidance and additional knowledge on what the avatar should say about this slide |
| Video: content / spoken text | Description and transcript of embedded videos. Lines with a timestamp like `[1:05] …` let the avatar jump to that point in the video |

Changes to several slides are collected – edited slides are marked with a dot in the thumbnail strip. Click **Save changes**; they apply from the next conversation. Slide images and order cannot be changed.

<Warning>
  Uploading a new file regenerates the content from the file – your edits are lost.
</Warning>

## Answering questions

<ParamField path="Questions beyond the presentation" type="Select">
  - **General knowledge allowed** (default): The presentation's content comes first. When the avatar answers from general knowledge, it says that this isn't from the presentation. It says openly when it doesn't know something for sure.
  - **Presentation content only**: The avatar answers only from slides, speaker notes and videos (and web search, if enabled). It politely declines other questions. Good for mandatory training.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Web search" type="Toggle">
  The avatar may look things up on the internet when a question needs current or external information, such as new regulations. It mentions that the answer comes from the internet. Off by default.
</ParamField>

## What viewers see

Viewers open the link and click **Start presentation**. The browser asks for the microphone. The avatar greets them and presents slide by slide. Viewers can:

- **interrupt and ask questions** at any time – the avatar jumps to the relevant slide if needed and then continues,
- **navigate themselves** (arrow keys, clicking the left/right edge of the slide or the thumbnails) – the avatar follows,
- **switch the language** by simply speaking English or German, or by asking for it.

The avatar announces embedded videos, plays them and summarizes them afterwards. While a video plays, the microphone is muted. When viewers ask where something is explained in a video, the avatar jumps to that point and plays it.

<Note>
  To jump within a video, the avatar needs a transcript with timestamps. It's created automatically on upload. For presentations uploaded before October 28, 2026, upload the file once more under **File**, or add the timestamps by hand in the **Slides** section.
</Note>

Add `?lang=en` or `?lang=de` to the link to set the interface language and the start language for that link.

## Protect access

<ParamField path="Link active" type="toggle">
  When the link is disabled, nobody can open the presentation. The link is kept and works again as soon as you re-enable it.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Password" type="optional">
  Viewers have to enter the password before they see any slides or the title. A browser then stays unlocked for seven days. If you change or remove the password, all viewers have to enter the new password.
</ParamField>

<Note>
  The link contains a random identifier that cannot be guessed. Without a password, anyone who knows the link can still open the presentation and start conversations.
</Note>

## Conversation minutes

Conversations with the avatar count towards your account's monthly **minute quota** – just like conversations with your agents. Usage appears in the usage overview under **Agents**. See [Pricing & Billing](/en/billing/plans).
