Under [Settings](https://avaluma.ai/studio/settings), at the bottom of the
Studio sidebar, you find three sections.

| Section | What you set there |
|---|---|
| **Language** | Interface language, **DE** or **EN**. Your browser stores the choice. |
| **Appearance** | **System** (follows your operating system), **Light** or **Dark**. |
| **Account** | Your email address and deleting the account. |

## Delete your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Delete your agents">
    An account can only be deleted once you no longer own any agents. While
    there are still some, the dialog shows how many and links to
    **Go to my agents**. To keep an agent, first
    [hand it over to another account](/en/agents/knowledge-tools#transfer-an-agent).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the deletion">
    Under **Account**, click **Delete account**, type your email address to
    confirm and click **Delete account permanently**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting cannot be undone. Your account and its data are removed, and you can
  no longer sign in with this email address.
</Warning>
