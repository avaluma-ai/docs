## How It Works

Avaluma AI has two components that work together to deliver a live avatar experience:

1. **Avatar Server** — A service that renders `.hvia` avatar files into a live video stream and publishes that stream into a LiveKit room.
2. **LiveKit Agent** — A Python worker that runs your voice AI pipeline (STT → LLM → TTS) and drives the avatar's audio input, making the avatar speak and react in real time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your license key and avatar file">
    Obtain your Avaluma license key and one or more `.hvia` avatar files from [avaluma.ai](https://avaluma.ai).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy the Avatar Server">
    Run the Avatar Server on a compatible machine. Place your `.hvia` files in the avatars folder and start the service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure and start a LiveKit Agent">
    Set your credentials, choose your avatar ID, and start the LiveKit Agent. It connects the voice AI pipeline to your avatar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a client">
    Open the [Avaluma Test Client](https://avaluma.ai/test-client) or the LiveKit Agent Playground to see your avatar live.
  </Step>
</Steps>
